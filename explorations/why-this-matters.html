Before everything else

Why This Matters

Before the infrastructure.
Before the systems. Before the theory.

There are three things worth sitting with.
They don't require any framework to feel.
You already know them.
You just may not have had words for them yet.

I of III

The Smoke Principle

Understanding is what makes things work.
Not data. Not process. Not documentation.
The inside of how things actually work — carried by people, transferred imperfectly, and gone the moment they leave.

"I know computers run on smoke."
"How do you know?"
"Because when the smoke leaves — it doesn't work anymore."

II of III

The Investment Principle

Every school ever built. Every road laid to last a century. Every story told to a child at bedtime. Humanity has always been trying to solve the smoke problem — instinctively, at enormous cost, without ever naming what it was doing.

Don't make them start from the smoke again.
That is what every one of these investments is saying.

III of III

The Value Principle

The most valuable thing we know cannot be fully transferred. A lifetime of understanding how things actually work has no adequate vessel. The fear of losing it is not irrational. It is precisely calibrated to the actual magnitude of what disappears.

It's a living thing. Precious and irreplaceable.
But we can point at it. And whisper about it.
We may be finding a way to pass it onwards.

We can't bottle the smoke. But we can learn to notice when the room goes cold.
It doesn't photograph well. But you know exactly when it's there.
The best it ever works is when it travels between us without either person noticing.
